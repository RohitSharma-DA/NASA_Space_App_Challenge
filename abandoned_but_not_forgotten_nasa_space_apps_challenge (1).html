CHAPTER 00 // WHEN EARTH LOOKED UPWARD

“For ten thousand generations we stood on the grass and wondered what the stars felt like. Then, out of aluminum, copper wire, and stubborn hope, we built messengers to go touch them for us.”

None of them could ever fly home. Every rover, lander, and camera left on the Moon and Mars was a one-way gift from human hands—resting quietly under alien skies so we might know we are not alone in the dark.

✦ OUR PROMISE TO THE PIONEERS

Why We Guard the Footprints We Left Behind

Every rover, lander, and descent stage resting on the Moon and Mars was left there because of the gentle, unforgiving math of spaceflight: every kilogram left on the surface meant more lunar stones carried home to Earth, or a softer touchdown for a rover carrying humanity’s eyes.

1967 OUTER SPACE TREATY

A vow among nations: every spacecraft launched from Earth remains forever part of our shared human story, protected wherever it comes to rest.

SACRED LANDING ZONES

NASA’s heritage guidelines establish quiet sanctuary perimeters around Tranquility Base and historic sites so future landers never disturb the first bootprints.
